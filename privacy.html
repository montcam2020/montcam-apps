English

The MontCam apps (Cartinhas, Barulhinhos, Estourinhos, Parzinhos and Pecinhas) are made by MontCam. We respect your privacy.

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • The apps have no accounts, no sign-in and no ads.
  • The apps have no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics.
  • The apps work offline. Sounds and voices stay on your device.
  • Children: the apps do not ask for or collect information from children.

Questions about this policy: montcam2020@gmail.com.
